Biography

Erzsébet

Erzsébet is a symphonic black metal band from Barcelona, inspired by the legend of Countess Erzsébet Báthory. The project combines aggressive black metal, orchestral arrangements, haunting melodies and a strongly theatrical visual identity.

The band's first release, The Blasphemous Lady, arrived in 2021 through Art Gates Records. In 2024, Erzsébet expanded its conceptual and cinematic scope with the full-length album Six Hundred & Fifty, a work built around the mythology surrounding Elisabeth Báthory. The album includes Zoe Marie Federoff (ex-Cradle of Filth) on “Domina Vestra.”

On stage, Erzsébet translates its music into an immersive performance in which sound, image and theatricality form part of the same narrative.
