04 / BAND

ERZSÉBET

Erzsébet is a symphonic black metal band from Barcelona, formed in 2020 by musicians with backgrounds in Perennial Isolation, Lvx in Tenebris, Acid Blood and Archetype X.

Inspired by the legend of Countess Erzsébet Báthory, the band combines ferocious black metal, cinematic orchestration and theatrical performance into a dark, immersive identity.
