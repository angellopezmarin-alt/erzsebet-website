The recorded works of Erzsébet

EP · 2021

The Blasphemous Lady

Art Gates Records

The Blasphemous Lady marked Erzsébet's debut in 2021, introducing the band's blend of raw black metal, symphonic arrangements and gothic theatricality. Built around the mythology of Countess Elisabeth Báthory, the six-track EP moves between aggressive tremolo-driven passages, blast beats and slower, brooding sections, creating a sinister and melancholic atmosphere. International metal media highlighted its strong melodies, varied vocals and deliberately rough-edged production, establishing the foundations of Erzsébet's dark and cinematic identity.
